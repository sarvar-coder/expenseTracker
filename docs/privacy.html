Xarajatlar — Maxfiylik siyosati

Yangilangan: 2026-10-02

Qanday ma'lumot yig'iladi

  • Hisob: elektron pochta va parol (Supabase orqali kirish uchun).
  • Xarajatlar: summa, tavsif, kategoriya, sana, oila a'zoligi va oylik byudjet. Ular qurilmada saqlanadi va Supabase serveriga sinxronlanadi.
  • Yozilgan yoki aytilgan matn: xarajatni ajratish uchun Google Gemini sun'iy intellektiga yuboriladi. Bepul tarifda Google bu matndan o'z modellarini o'qitish uchun foydalanishi mumkin.
  • Mikrofon: faqat ovozli kiritishda, nutq qurilmaning o'zida matnga aylantiriladi. Ovoz yozuvlari saqlanmaydi va yuborilmaydi.
  • Bildirishnomalar: qurilmaning Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) tokeni hisobingizga bog'lab Supabase'da saqlanadi va faqat oila bildirishnomalarini yuborish uchun ishlatiladi. Hisobdan chiqqanda o'chiriladi.

Kimlar ko'radi

Oila a'zolari oiladagi barcha xarajatlaringizni ko'radi (maxfiy xarajatlar yo'q). Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.

O'chirish

Hisobingiz va barcha ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirish uchun iam.developer.yu@gmail.com manziliga xat yozing. 30 kun ichida o'chiriladi.

Xarajatlar — Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-02

What we collect

  • Account: email and password (sign-in via Supabase).
  • Expenses: amount, description, category, date, family membership and monthly budget. Stored on the device and synced to Supabase.
  • Typed or spoken text: sent to Google Gemini AI to parse the expense. On the free tier Google may use this text to improve its models.
  • Microphone: used only for voice input; speech is converted to text on the device. Audio is never stored or uploaded.
  • Notifications: the device's Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) token is stored in Supabase with your account and used only to send family notifications. It is deleted when you sign out.

Who sees it

Family members see all your family expenses (there are no private expenses). Data is not sold or used for ads.

Deletion

To delete your account and all data, email iam.developer.yu@gmail.com. Deleted within 30 days.
